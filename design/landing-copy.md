## 2. The week (how it works)
H2: A week on Orba looks like this.
- Monday. Set up the board before standup. Start from a template or name your own columns, give the project a colour and a due date, and invite the team by email.
- Midweek. Everything about a task lives on its card. Priority, due date, labels and an owner on the front; the description, comments and files one click behind it.
- Friday. Drag it to Done and the dashboard keeps count. Each project shows how much is done and how much is still open, so nobody has to ask where things stand.

## 3. The card
H2: Every card carries its own story.
Body: Open a card and the whole conversation is there: what needs doing, who owns it, when it is due, and the files people dropped on it (up to 10 MB each). The counts sit on the card face, so nothing gets buried.

## 4. The team
H2: Invite people by email and give them a role.
Body: Owners, admins and members each see what they need. Someone without an account gets a one-click sign-up link and lands straight on the board.

## 5. Pricing
H2: Free for three boards. One flat price after that.
- Free: USD 0, forever. 3 boards, 3 people per board, every card feature.
- Pro: USD 10 a month, billed yearly (or 12 monthly). For the whole team, not per person. Unlimited boards and people, Pro templates.
