# Orba landing rework: brief

## What it is
Orba (orba.work) is a Kanban board for small teams: projects with custom columns, cards with priority, due date, labels, an assignee, comments and files (up to 10 MB), drag and drop, email invitations with owner / admin / member roles, templates, a dashboard with progress per project. Light and dark themes. Nathan's own product.

## Who visits
A lead or founder of a small product team (3 to 15 people) who is tired of heavy tools (Jira) or of a board nobody keeps up to date. They compare against Trello, Linear, Notion boards.

Objections: "another board tool", "will my team actually use it", "how much per seat", "is it a toy".

## The one action
Start free (register). Secondary: see pricing.

## Real facts (from the code, 2026-11-25)
- Free: 3 boards, 3 members per board, forever, no card.
- Pro: USD 10 a month billed yearly (120), or 12 monthly. Flat for the whole team, not per person. Unlimited boards and people, Pro templates.
- Cards: priority, due date, labels, assignee, description, comments, attachments up to 10 MB.
- Roles: owner, admin, member. Invites by email, one-click sign-up link for people without an account.
- New projects start with To Do, In Progress, Done, or a template.

No customers, logos, testimonials or usage metrics exist. None are invented.

## Current site weaknesses
White, stone greys, Geist, monochrome: tidy but forgettable. Nothing says "Orba" rather than any Kanban. The board visual is grey on grey, so the product (colour-coded, moving cards) looks lifeless.

## Decisions (round 1, 2026-11-25)
- Scope: landing first, the rest of the marketing pages follow the system.
- Brand: free rein (rebrand proposal, mark included).
- Mood: playful and colourful.
- Hero visual: the real board UI.
